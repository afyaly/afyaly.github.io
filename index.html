What Afyaly does

The clinical, operational, and financial work of a facility in one record.

Clinical

Triage, OPD, IPD

Kenya Triage Category queues, structured SOAP notes, ward rounds, growth charts, MCH book, immunisations. Every order, vital, and prescription on one Encounter.

Pharmacy

Dispensing & formulary

KEML formulary, drug-drug interactions, pregnancy categories. Dispense queue with stock, batch, and expiry. Two-signature narcotics register.

Laboratory

Sample to result

Barcoded samples, analyser worklists over HL7 v2 (Mindray, Sysmex, Beckman, Roche), validation and release, turnaround-time reporting.

Imaging

Modality worklist

DICOM modality worklist, radiologist reporting, PACS retrieval. X-Ray, ultrasound, CT, MRI.

Billing

Charges, bills, claims

Charge capture from every service. Cashier shift close-out. SHA eClaim packaging with clinical attachments. Private insurer claims. eTIMS receipts.

Reporting

DHIS2 & statutory

MOH-105, 705, 710, 731 monthly summaries compiled from clinical data. Notifiable-disease notices. Daily ops dashboards for facility admin.

LindaMama · An Afyaly product

A maternity record the mother owns — that works at any facility.

The MCH booklet she carries today is the part of maternity care that already works. LindaMama is its digital companion: ANC visits, lab and ultrasound results across clinics, delivery, and the KEPI immunisation schedule on a record she keeps. Facilities scan a QR to read or write — including the ones that don't run any HMS at all.
