About Afyaly

Software that fits a Kenyan facility the way it actually works.

A health facility is not a retailer and not a school. It's a regulated clinical-care unit with a licensed catalogue of services, a workforce of regulated cadres, a longitudinal record that has to be retrievable years later, and a payer mix where the same service is priced differently per scheme. Afyaly is built around those facts.

Approach

One record, one product

Patient, Encounter, Order, Claim — four primitives. Everything else hangs off them. The same codebase serves a single-room dispensary and a national referral hospital.

Compliance

SHA, DHIS2, KRA — first class

eClaims, MOH monthly summaries, and eTIMS aren't bolt-ons. The data model carries the fields each integration needs from registration onward.

Your data

Your facility's data, exportable

Patient records, billing, claims, and reports stay yours. Full CSV and FHIR export, on demand. No vendor lock-in if you decide to leave.

Where Afyaly is in its lifecycle

The product is in early implementation. Design is complete; the first facilities go live during 2026. We are looking for design partners — facilities that will run the system day-one and shape the workflows it ships with.
